Preferred sizes:

- Portrait category/hero images: `1600x2000` or `2000x2500`
- Horizontal section images: `1800x1200`
- Format: `.webp`
- Quality: `75-85`

Before wiring into the site, check:

- No fake client logos.
- No fake certifications.
- No readable fake text.
- No watermarks.
- No unsafe or messy production environment.
- No misleading factory ownership signals.
